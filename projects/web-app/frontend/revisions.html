Of course! 🌱 Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food using sunlight. Here's the simple version:

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light Energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Plants take in carbon dioxide (CO₂) from the air and water (H₂O) from the soil. Using sunlight, they turn these into glucose (sugar for energy) and release oxygen (O₂) that we breathe!

Think of it like a recipe: sunlight + water + air = food for the plant! 🌞
