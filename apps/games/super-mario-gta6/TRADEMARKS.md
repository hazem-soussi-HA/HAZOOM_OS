This project does not reproduce any GTA® artwork, mission design,
narrative, character likeness, soundtrack, or code. The "GTA-style"
driving mode (cars on roads, "F" to enter a vehicle) is a generic
gameplay pattern that predates the GTA® series and is referenced here
as a tribute to open-world game design in general.

The use of "GTA" in the project name is an homage and is in no way
intended to suggest endorsement, sponsorship, or association with
Take-Two Interactive or Rockstar Games.
