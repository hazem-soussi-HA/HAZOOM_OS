## 1. What This Project Is

A non-commercial, open-source fan game that pays homage to two
gameplay traditions your companies helped define:

  • The 2D side-scrolling platformer (Nintendo® lineage)
  • The open-world driving mini-game (Rockstar® lineage)

The project is offered free of charge. It contains no copied
Nintendo® or Rockstar® assets — every sprite is drawn at runtime
from mathematical primitives, and every sound is synthesized in
real time. The project ships entirely as original source code
under the MIT License.

The full architecture, design notes, and legal posture are
documented in the repository:

  • `README.md` — project overview
  • `LICENSE` — MIT grant + statement of what is and isn't
    original code
  • `TRADEMARKS.md` — full ® and ™ attribution to your marks
  • `PRE_PARTNERSHIP_STATEMENT.md` — my personal statement of
    intent and respect
  • `AUDIT_REPORT.md` — independent technical audit and roadmap
  • `mario_gta6_2d.py` and `website/` — the actual game

## 2. Compliance With Your Published Guidelines

This project is designed to remain in compliance with Nintendo's
*Game Content Guidelines for Fan Works*:

  ✅ **Free to play.** No price, no ads, no IAP, no premium tier
  ✅ **Original assets.** Zero copied sprites, sounds, or music
  ✅ **Clearly unofficial.** The required "not affiliated"
     notice appears in the in-game credits, on the website
     footer, in `TRADEMARKS.md`, and in this document
  ✅ **Will comply with takedown.** See § 4 below
  ✅ **Original code under permissive license.** MIT — your
     legal team can read, fork, or reuse any part of it without
     restriction

## 4. Takedown Procedure

If your team would like this project modified, renamed, or
removed, the fastest path is:

  1. **Email:** Open a GitHub issue at
     <https://github.com/hazem-soussi-HA/mario_gta6/issues> with
     the tag `[LEGAL]` in the title. I monitor the repo
     regularly.
  2. **Email directly:** I am reachable through the contact
     address listed on my GitHub profile
     <https://github.com/hazem-soussi-HA>.
  3. **DMCA / formal notice:** If a formal takedown notice is
     required for your internal records, the same GitHub issue
     channel can be used, and I will respond within **72 hours**.

Once a request is received:

  • The project will be renamed or taken down within **7 days**
    of confirmation, or sooner if you prefer
  • The repository will remain publicly archived (read-only) with
    a redirect to a renamed fork, so that any existing users are
    not stranded
  • No public commentary will be made about the takedown — your
    legal team's preferences on this will be respected

## 5. Why I Built This

I built this project because I love the craft of making games and
because both *Super Mario* and *Grand Theft Auto* are foundational
to why I became a developer. I am not the first person to feel
this way, and I am grateful that the games industry has a long
history of fan projects becoming official collaborations, when
the IP holders choose to engage.

I would be honored to be part of that tradition. I would also be
equally happy to keep building, in private, in compliance with
your guidelines, and to ship original work of my own over the
coming years.

Either path is the right one for your companies, and I will
support whichever you choose.
