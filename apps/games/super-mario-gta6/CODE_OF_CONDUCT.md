# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic background, or religion.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the project and community
- Show empathy towards others

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Plagiarism or unauthorized use of copyrighted material
- Attempting to compromise project security or legal protections

## Enforcement

Violations may result in temporary or permanent ban from the project.
All enforcement decisions are made by the project lead, Hazem Soussi (HA).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces including issues, pull
requests, commits, and any other project communication.

## Contact

For violations or concerns, contact @hazem_soussi-HA directly.
