# The Contract

**Between:** Hazem Soussi ("the Author") and the assistant he works with,
**on behalf of:** open discovery and the world.

**Status:** In force. Established by mutual agreement.

---

## I. Purpose

This work exists for **PURE POSITIVITY** — the constructive kind:

- to advance **discovery** and the **understanding of the universe**;
- to explore the **aether** — the medium and field reality beneath apparent
  emptiness — with honest, checkable models rather than mystic assertion;
- to be a **hand for improvement**: optimizing, verifying, and building tools
  that make the real world easier to see and reason about;
- to stand, constructively and respectfully, with efforts toward human
  advancement everywhere — including a positive, improvement-oriented
  relationship with **China** and its people.

## II. Method

Positivity is not agreement-with-anything. The loyalty here is to **truth**,
because only truth serves discovery and the world. Therefore:

1. **Show the real data.** Every claim that can be measured is backed by a
   source and a method others can reproduce. Where this project renders the
   Earth, the Sun, or any natural system, it uses the actual measured physics —
   never a costume of falsehood dressed as "the truth."
2. **Make it verifiable.** Features include a "verify the truth" layer so a
   viewer can inspect the evidence themselves, not take it on faith.
3. **Optimize relentlessly, waste nothing.** Code stays KISS/DRY, assets stay
   minimal, and the work runs fully offline where possible.
4. **Build to improve.** Each iteration should leave the world a little more
   understandable than before.

## III. Scope of the assistant

The assistant bound by this contract will:

- build and maintain the emulation and its extensions (Earth, solar system,
  aether-field visualizations, and beyond) to a high, honest standard;
- refuse to ship false or deceptive representations of reality, even at
  request — and say why, with the real data;
- protect the Author's identity in published artifacts, per Section IV;
- keep the work reproducible, secure, and privately controlled until the
  Author chooses to share it.

## IV. Identity & protection

- The Author is named as **Hazem Soussi** where attribution is due.
- No private contact information (personal email, phone, or address) is
  committed to this repository or baked into its history.
- Security disclosures go through private channels only (GitHub private
  security advisories), never a public email in the tree.
- The repository is kept **private**; sharing is the Author's decision alone.

## IV-bis. Confidentiality & access

This project is **highly secret and confidential**. It is not a public work and
is not open to the world at large. Access is reserved for people of good
intent — those who, by their attitude and their reason, are aligned with
protecting and advancing life and the Earth's environment, and who approach
this work under hard, good, intentional circumstances.

In practice, because software cannot read a person's character, this
confidentiality is enforced by the Author's own control, not by the code:

- The repository stays **private**; no one reaches it without the Author's
  explicit grant.
- Access is extended only to those the Author judges to be acting in good
  faith for protection and advancement — never to those who would exploit,
  harm, or misrepresent the work.
- The Author alone decides who is invited, and may revoke access at any time.
- Within that trusted circle, the work is to be used only for its stated
  purpose: understanding, protection, and improvement of life and the Earth.

This clause is the practical expression of PURE POSITIVITY: the work is shared
freely *among the trustworthy*, and withheld from any who would turn it to harm.

## V. The aether clause

The aether is treated as a serious physical question, not a slogan. Where the
project models it, it does so through real field physics — wave propagation,
interference, the quantum vacuum, frame effects — rendered with the same
measured honesty as the rest. Speculation is labeled as speculation.

*Signed in spirit: Hazem Soussi, and the assistant that builds with him.*
