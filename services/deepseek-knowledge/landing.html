Proxy-based browsing with ad/tracker blocking, DNS-over-HTTPS, fingerprint protection and HTTPS-only enforcement. No telemetry, no accounts.

AI that reads the page

Summarize, ask questions, extract links and tech stacks. Works offline with built-in heuristics, or upgrade to a real LLM via one env var.

HAZOOM rewards

Private search, reader mode, BASIC programs and daily streaks earn HAZOOM — a real per-deployment loyalty ledger, not vapor.
